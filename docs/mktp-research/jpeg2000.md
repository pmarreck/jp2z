# JPEG 2000 (J2K/J2C codestream and JP2 wrapper): MKTP research record

Retraceable account of how far jp2z's strict validation reaches toward
Maximum Known Technically Possible (MKTP) corruption detection, as the
term is defined in Validate's `TERMINOLOGY.md` B4: the payload is consumed
to its declared end, every known objective invariant is enforced, the
residual blind class is documented and the evidence is replayed against an
oracle the author did not write. This record indexes evidence; it does not
grant an MKTP ledger row. A passing mutation percentage is not proof of
completeness, and a listed reach is never evidence of corruption.

Entries are dated and appended; earlier results stay as they were
measured, labelled stale when superseded.

## 1. Scope and identity (2026-10-09)

- Format: ITU-T T.800 / ISO/IEC 15444-1 Part 1 codestreams (`.j2k`,
  `.j2c`) and the JP2 file format of Annex I (`.jp2`). Part 2 (JPX,
  arbitrary decompositions, Rsiz bit 15) and Part 15 (HTJ2K, Rsiz bit 14
  or cblksty bit 6) are recognised and reported as valid-but-unverified
  (finding 145), never decoded, never failed for being unsupported.
- Variants covered end to end: 5/3 reversible and 9/7 irreversible
  wavelets; all five progression orders; POC (main and tile-part);
  tile-parts (TPsot/TNsot, interleaved tiles); COD/COC/QCD/QCC/RGN main
  and first-tile-part overrides; precincts; SOP/EPH; PPM/PPT packed
  headers; TLM, PLM, PLT length cross-checks; code-block styles BYPASS,
  RESET, TERMALL, VSC, PTERM, SEGSYM; 1 to 16384 components with
  per-component precision, signedness and sub-sampling; JP2 boxes
  signature, ftyp, jp2h (ihdr, bpcc, colr, pclr, cmap, cdef), jp2c.
- Source identity: `yolo` at 54917d6 for every indexed experiment; 3953e2a
  adds the B.3 check recorded in sections 3 and 4.4. Binary: `zig-out/bin/jp2z` built from that
  tree with Zig 0.16.0 (ReleaseFast default; `MUTE_DEBUG_STATUS` has no
  effect on a release build).
- Oracle: OpenJPEG 2.5.4 (`opj_decompress`, `opj_dump`, the C library
  behind `src/ffi/openjpeg_wrapper.zig`), from the project's Nix flake.
  Fixture corpus: `openjpeg-data` at 39524bd (flake input), 57 ISO
  15444-4 conformance files under `input/conformance` and 226 files under
  `input/nonregression`. In-repo copies of 26 conformance fixtures plus
  one real-encoder JP2 are hashed in `jpeg2000-fixtures.sha256`.
- Consumers: jpegz pins jp2z 54917d6 (`build.zig.zon`); Validate reaches
  jp2z through jpegz and lists `jpeg2000` as witnessed `full` on its 10
  labelled-good files with no MKTP variant row.

### Hostile-input safety

Every structural failure above returns before geometry is used; 16-bit
and 32-bit arithmetic that could overflow on legal values is widened
(numTilesXY, tileRect, TLM/PLM varints). The in-core mutation matrix runs
in-process so a panic fails the suite. The 2026-09-16 and 09-18 probes
recorded no crash across 27 fixtures × 400 trials (section 4).

## 3. Research trail

- 2026-09-06: severity policy written down (LEARNINGS.md): undecodable
  values FAIL, reserved-but-ignorable WARN, defined-but-unimplemented 145.
  POC REpoc/CEpoc bounds deliberately not checked (B.12.1.3 clamps).
- 2026-09-12: BDFN ruling, another decoder's tolerance never downgrades a
  proven nonconformance. Applied to TPsot ≥ TNsot (FAIL kept; Validate's
  downgrade request withdrawn 2026-09-19) and to COD Scod / cblksty
  reserved bits under Part 1.
- 2026-09-16: Annex E exponent relation. Hypothesis: E-10 ties reversible
  QCD exponents to precision and gain, so a mismatch is a FAIL. Rejected
  by the text (E.2 is informative) and by data (ISO p0_10, file1, file9
  exceed the equality). Landed as the inequality budget, WARN 260 only.
  Boundary: an 8→9 bit Ssiz change on p0_13 is undetectable by any
  codestream cross-check; 12-bit trips 260.
- 2026-09-16: absent-tile rule. Inflated Xsiz/Ysiz mutants declared tiles
  the stream never delivered; ISO b2_mono legitimately omits 9 of 25
  tiles because every omitted tile is empty in its 5×3 sub-sampled
  component (B.3/B.6). Rule: absent tile with samples FAIL, absent empty
  tile clean.
- 2026-10-09 (this entry): SIZ tile-origin constraint. Inventory of
  parseSizBody against Table A.9 found the grid guard checked
  XTOsiz ≤ XOsiz but not XTOsiz + XTsiz > XOsiz (B.3). Experiment 4.4
  below. Implemented red-first as FAIL 146 with the clause in the detail.
  Candidates found by the same inventory and not implemented tonight:
  ihdr-first and colr-required in jp2h (need the Annex I text; the
  test helpers build colr-less JP2s and would all have to change), MCT
  with fewer than 3 or geometrically mismatched components (needs G.2
  text; decode already refuses), COD-family markers in a non-first
  tile-part header (needs the Table A.1 placement text). Each is a
  recognised-but-unverified reach today, not evidence of corruption.

## 4. Experiments (indexed; artifacts immutable)

### 4.1 Conformance decode sweep (`./sweep`, conformance/SCORECARD.md, sweep.ndjson)

57 ISO 15444-4 files decoded by jp2z and compared to opj_decompress:
PASS 33 byte-exact, NEAR 4 (max_abs ≤ 1 on 9/7), FAIL 0, skip 20 (oracle
output not directly comparable: sub-sampled, signed or dimension
mismatch), ERROR/CRASH/TIMEOUT 0. Deterministic and machine-independent
(integer decoder, fixed oracle). Current at 54917d6.

### 4.2 In-core mutation matrix (`tests/mutation_matrix.zig`, conformance/MUTATION_SCORECARD.md)

Runs inside `./test`. 26 pristine controls (23 codestreams, 3 JP2) must
validate clean; known-invalid mutations of SOC at three scales must FAIL.
Five entropy-body classes at the quarter-point offset with seeded
streams (BLAKE3 of a per-fixture label into ChaCha): sniper (1 bit),
bolter (1 byte XOR 0xFF), legacy zero-fill 1024 B (kept under its legacy
label), shotgun (8..16 distinct bits in a 32-byte window, the fleet's
2026-09-29 definition), nuke (1024-byte pseudorandom overwrite; fleet
default 4096, 1024 here because the smallest bodies are shorter).
Measured floors at 11ac8af, codestream family: 15 / 16 / 21 / 19 / 22 of
23; JP2 family 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 3. Columns are not comparable across
operators.

### 4.3 corruption-probe (conformance/PROBE_COVERAGE.md, conformance/probe/<fixture>/)

External tool `corruption-probe` (sibling project, mutation v1, BLAKE3-XOF
DRBG seed 0x1234), validator `jp2z validate --strict`, exit map
2=warning, pristine copy exit 0 required. 27 fixtures × 100 rounds × 4
modes (sniper, bolter, legacy dense "shotgun" of min(4096, size/8) bytes
floor 64, truncation) at 449eb1d (2026-09-16). Shotgun and truncation
rejected 100% everywhere except p1_04's comment window (57 survivors).
Sniper and bolter survivors labelled on 2026-09-18 by `./probe-label`
(replay each accepted trial, decode with OpenJPEG, compare to jp2z's own
pristine decode): 1103 accepted, 266 inert, 776 changed (true false
negatives, 14.8% of the 5245 non-comment trials), 4 wrapper refusals.
Changed by region: 758 packet data, 13 main header, 4 tile-part header,
1 JP2 box. The 18 header bytes: 14 QCD step-size bytes, 2 Ssiz sign bits,
1 pclr entry, 1 other; none has a codestream cross-check. Mode counts
are per fixture in the PROBE_COVERAGE table. Stale with respect to the
fleet's 2026-09-29 vocabulary; a rerun waits for corruption_probe v2 and
will be recorded with its version and parameters.

### 4.4 SIZ tile-origin constraint (2026-10-09, this record)

Purpose: does jp2z name a SIZ whose first tile ends at or before the
image origin, and does OpenJPEG agree?

Fixture: in-repo `c1_mono.j2c` (sha256
7ed01bd12e2123579b3177200162c9f6b684dc9ee92585cc4f89d2a7cd9e9b50,
303×179, one tile at (0,0)). Mutants by direct byte write (stream offset
16 = XOsiz, 24 = XTsiz): `illegal_50` XOsiz 100, XTsiz 50 (sha256
f2f23d612a7c3b29adfc52c3d7e9fd8608c3570988b6567f54ef7bc7bb8e3752);
`illegal_eq` XOsiz 100, XTsiz 100 (boundary, equality is illegal; sha256
a0beab1fa9d0c243eefec6217a3b14e0094b9d7a5bdd742df4eb422e9de00113);
`legal_offset` XOsiz 100, XTsiz 303 (legal grid; sha256
73e7700a1633a973ba4ca1d7c43487c6f1a6fcbf81cac57973717b11936e37bd).

Commands: `jp2z validate --strict FILE` (binary built from 54917d6, then
from the fix), `opj_decompress -i FILE -o OUT.pgm` (OpenJPEG 2.5.4).

Before the fix: pristine exit 0 (info 208, 254). `illegal_50` FAIL on
downstream symptoms only (5 of 7 tiles absent, numbps=0 with passes,
over/under-read), `illegal_eq` FAIL (3 of 4 tiles absent), no finding
named the SIZ. OpenJPEG rejected both at the SIZ marker: "Error with SIZ
marker: illegal tile offset". `legal_offset` decodes in OpenJPEG; jp2z
reports 251/252 because moving the image origin on a real codestream
changes the packet geometry the data was encoded for (a confound of the
mutation, not a disagreement about the SIZ rule).

After the fix: `illegal_50` and `illegal_eq` FAIL 146 at the SIZ marker
with the B.3 detail; the 27 in-repo fixtures and the 57 conformance
files produce no such finding (0 false positives). Unit test "validate:
SIZ tile grid must reach the image origin" is a classifier over both
axes and both sides of the boundary (XTsiz/YTsiz ∈ {1, 2, 3, 6} on a 4×4
image at origin (2,2)) plus the c1_mono mutant and the pristine file; it
failed before the implementation (196 pass, 1 fail) and passes after.

Differential over `input/nonregression` (151 of 226 files matched by
extension j2k/j2c/jp2/jpx/jpc): OpenJPEG says "illegal tile offset" on
three files, `issue427-illegal-tile-offset.jp2` (sha256
369a21da770aa2da0b2cf6742087db4d5ba4e32a62f41f7ea2c6f038c060812f, 182
bytes), `edf_c2_1002767.jp2` and `edf_c2_101463.jp2`. jp2z fails all
three with 146 from the pre-existing SIZ guard: their XTOsiz exceeds XOsiz
(4294967292, 64 and 1024 against an origin of 0), the other half of the
B.3 pair, which jp2z already failed before this entry. Verdicts agree
on every file and the new rule adds no disagreement; no other file
trips either tool's tile-offset check.

## 5. Outcomes

- Clean-corpus acceptance unchanged: 57/57 conformance files accepted
  (33 byte-exact + 4 NEAR + 20 skip in decode; no SIZ finding in strict
  validation), 26/26 in-core controls, 27/27 probe pristines.
- New must-detect class: SIZ grids that miss the image origin. Two
  synthetic mutants and one third-party regression file FAIL at the
  marker; before, two of them failed only on symptoms and one was already
  failed by an earlier guard.
- Crashes, timeouts, resource exhaustion: none in any experiment above.
- Nothing in this entry changes a measured percentage; the probe tables
  stay as measured on 2026-09-16/18.

## 6. Residuals (format-level, not implementation reaches)

JPEG 2000 Part 1 carries no checksum over packets or headers. The
following mutations produce codestreams that are fully self-consistent
and are therefore undetectable without external integrity information:

- Entropy-coded bytes whose change keeps every segment inside its byte
  budget (within the 12-byte over-read cap, 3 with PTERM; SEGSYM and
  TERMALL streams detect more). 758 of the 776 labelled false negatives.
- QCD/QCC exponents and mantissas on 9/7 streams, and reversible
  exponents above the E-10 budget (the standard permits them).
- SIZ precision, signedness and sub-sampling changes on a raw codestream
  (the JP2 wrapper adds the ihdr/bpcc cross-check).
- Comment bodies, ICC profile bytes, palette entries, CRG values.
- Semantically equivalent encodings (empty-packet bit forms, oversize
  tile grids that still mean one tile, progression order changes on a
  one-layer one-component stream).

Implementation reaches (ours, not the format's): Part 2 and HTJ2K
codestreams (145), the three disputed rows in section 2, the decode
refusal of tile-level COD/COC changes to decomposition or wavelet.

## 7. Independent review

- Oracle: OpenJPEG 2.5.4 throughout (sweep, probe labelling, this
  entry's differential). Disagreements recorded: OpenJPEG decodes TPsot ≥
  TNsot streams and ignores the field (jp2z FAIL, ruling stands);
  OpenJPEG decodes the mixed-precision p0_13 mutants that the packed
  Image contract refuses (4 wrapper refusals, a limit of the Image type);
  OpenJPEG's "illegal tile offset" fires where jp2z's earlier guard
  already failed (same verdict, different label).
- A third decoder (Kakadu, JasPer or Grok) has been used only ad hoc
  (JasPer on surplus passes); no systematic three-way run exists.
- Unverified claims: the four clause readings marked D or M in section 2
  rest on memory of the standard and on OpenJPEG's behaviour, not on the
  text in front of the author. BDFN holds the ITU PDF; the verbatim
  extracts in `docs/` cover Annex E and the profile tables only.
